# TowerLine — Hagaha Bandhigga Wasaaradda (Soomaali)

**Alaabta:** Wasaaradda Isgaarsiinta iyo Technology — Nidaamka Maamulka iyo Kormeerka Tawarrada  
**Magaca shaqada:** TowerLine  
**Loogu talagalay:** Wasaaradda Isgaarsiinta iyo Technology (MoCIT), Somaliland  
**Dhagaystayaasha:** Wasiirada, agaasimayaasha, iyo shaqaalaha farsamada ee eegaya demo / bilowga tijaabada  
**Xaaladda:** Marxaladda 3 waa dhammaatay (toddobaadyada 13–29). Codsiyada goobaha ee dadweynaha iyo silsiladda HQ ayaa ku jira demo-ga.

Isticmaal faylkan sidii qoraal hadal iyo qaab slides ah markaad nidaamka tooska ah u bandhigayso.

---

## 1. Furitaanka (2 daqiiqo)

**Yiraah:**

> TowerLine waa nidaamka rasmiga ah ee Wasaaradda loogu talagalay in lagu diiwaangeliyo, khariidadiyo, kormeero, laguna ruqsadeeyo tawarrada isgaarsiinta iyo baahinta (TV/cable) ee Somaliland oo dhan. Khariidadda, diiwaanka, kormeerada goobaha, codsiyada goobaha ee dadweynaha, waraaqaha oggolaanshaha, frequenci-yada, iyo warbixinnada daabacan waxay ku midaysan yihiin meel keliya — Ingiriisi iyo Soomaali.

**Farriinta muhiimka ah:** Hal nidaam oo loogu talagalay shaqada maalinlaha, warbixinaha tooska ah, iyo raadraaca u hoggaansanaanta.

---

## 2. Dhibaatada aan xalinayno (2 daqiiqo)

Haddii aanay jirin nidaam midaysan, shaqaalaha wasaaraddu waxay inta badan ku tiirsan yihiin spreadsheet-yo, waraaqo, iyo sawirro kala firidsan. Taasi waxay adkaynaysaa in la jawaabo:

- Tawarradu meeye, yaa hawlgeliya?
- Goobee u baahan kormeer ama fiiro degdeg ah?
- Oggolaanshee frequenci ayaa dhawaan dhacaya?
- Soo-gudbintee kormeere ayaa sugaya oggolaanshaha wasaaradda?
- Yaa wax beddelay, goorma?
- Codsi goobeed cusub ma la xilsaaray, ma la booqday, ma la oggolaaday?

**TowerLine waxay ka jawaabtaa su’aalahan hal shaashad.**

## 4. Bandhigga tooska ah (12–14 daqiiqo)

Fur: `http://127.0.0.1:8001` (ama URL-ka tijaabada marka la martigeliyo).

### Tallaabo A — Maamulaha wasaaradda

1. Bogga gelitaanka, dooro **Gal degdeg ah → Maamulaha wasaaradda**.
2. Fur **Dashboard** — tus tirada tawarrada, goobaha xasaasiga ah, kormeerada dib u dhacay, oggolaanshaha sugaya, iyo frequenci-yada dhacaya.
3. Fur **Khariidadda** — midabeé calaamadaha shirkad; ku shaandhee gobol; guji tower; isticmaal **Daabac / sawir**.
4. Fur **Diiwaanka tawarrada** — tus degmo, ilaha korontada, iyo raadinta.
5. Fur **Warbixinnada** — fur diiwaanka guud, tus daabacaadda, sheeg Excel.
6. Fur **Frequenci** — tus oggolaanshaha firfircoon / dhacaya.
7. Fur **Isticmaalayaasha** iyo **Diiwaanka falalka** — tus cidda wax arki karta, iyo in isbeddellada la diiwaangeliyo.

### Tallaabo B — Kormeeraha gobolka

1. Ka bax, kadib dooro **Kormeere · Maroodi Jeex**.
2. Tus in khariidadda iyo liisku ay yihiin gobolkaas oo keliya.
3. Fur tower → **Diiwaangeli kormeer** — tus goobaha ikhtiyaariga iyo booqasho faallo keliya.
4. Tus **Soo-gudbintayda** — booqashadu waa sugaysaa ilaa wasaaraddu oggolaato.

### Tallaabo C — Maamulaha hawlgalka

1. Gal **Maamulaha hawlgalka** (`ops@mocit.local`).
2. Fur **Oggolaanshaha**, oggolow kormeerka, xaqiiji inuu noqday mid toos ah.
3. Tus in **Isticmaalayaasha** iyo **Diiwaanka falalka** aysan u furnaan doorarkan.

### Tallaabo D — Luuqadda

Beddel **EN / SO** madaxa bogga si qolku u arko calaamadaha Soomaaliga.

### Tallaabo E — Codsiga goobta ee dadweynaha (silsiladda HQ)

Isticmaal faylasha Maroodi Jeex ee la beeray (marxalad kasta waa jirtaa). Furaha dhammaan akoonada: `password`.

1. Marti ahaan, fur **Guidelines** (`/guidelines`) kadib **Apply** (`/apply`). Gelid lama baahna. Sheeg in lambarka raadraaca uu soo baxo kadib gudbinta.
2. Gal **Madaxa Qaybta**. Fur **Codsiyada**. Tus **Demo Maroodi Jeex Site** (la helay) iyo **Demo assigned Maroodi site**.
3. Gal **Isku-duwaha Maroodi Jeex**. Fur faylka la xilsaaray. Booqashada, faallooyinka, iyo saxeexa ayaa u dira Agaasimaha (ama soo celiya).
4. **Agaasimaha Waaxda:** **Demo director review Maroodi site** wuxuu sugayaa Haa/Maya. **Demo refused Maroodi site** wuxuu muujiyaa in **Maya uu xidho faylka**.
5. **Agaasimaha Guud:** **Demo DG review Maroodi site** wuxuu sugayaa Oggolaansho ama Celin. **Demo granted Maroodi site** waa tower toos ah oo leh warqad. Daabac nuqullada HQ iyo shirkadda. Magacyada iyo saxeexyadu waxay ka muuqdaan faylka, tower-ka, iyo warqadda.

## 5. Doorarka iyo amniga (2 daqiiqo)

| Doorka | Gelitaanka |
|---|---|
| **Maamulaha wasaaradda** | Nidaamka oo dhan: tawarro, waraaqo, frequenci, warbixino, isticmaalayaal, diiwaanka falalka, codsiyo |
| **Maamulaha hawlgalka** | Isla shaqada maamulaha marka laga reebo isticmaalayaasha, diiwaanka falalka, dejinta, iyo codsiyada goobaha |
| **Kormeeraha gobolka** | Tawarrada iyo kormeerada gobol(lada) loo qoondeeyay; soo-gudbintu waxay sugtaa oggolaansho |
| **Madaxa Qaybta** | Codsiyada goobaha. Wuxuu xilsaaraa fayl la helay ama la soo celiyay |
| **Isku-duwaha gobolka** | Faylasha Maroodi Jeex ee la xilsaaray. Booqashada, kadibna u gudbi Agaasimaha ama soo celi |
| **Agaasimaha Waaxda** | Haa waxay u dirtaa Agaasimaha Guud. Maya waa xidhaa |
| **Agaasimaha Guud** | Oggolaansho (tower toos ah + warqad) ama ku celi Agaasimaha |
| **Daawade shirkadeed** | Loogu diyaariyay mustaqbalka; **la damiyay marxaladan** |

- Shaqaaluhu **isma diiwaangelin karaan** — maamuluhu ayaa sameeya akoonada.
- Isbeddel kasta oo muhiim ah waxaa lagu qoraa **diiwaanka falalka**.
- Khariidadda iyo liisaska ma muujiyaan tawarro ka baxsan xadka isticmaalaha.

## 6. Waxa *aan* ku jirin noocan (1 daqiiqo)

Si cad oo kalsooni leh u sheeg:

- Weli ma jiraan xog toos ah oo shirkadaha (SNMP/API)  
- Weli ma jiraan fariimo SMS ama iimayl oo xasuusin ah (kaliya dashboard)  
- Weli ma jirto khariidad dadweyne  
- Weli ma jiro app mobile gaar ah (web-ka mobile ayaa shaqeeya goobta)  
- Soo dejinta liiska dhabta ah waxay sugaysaa faylka spreadsheet ee MoCIT  
- Liiska rasmiga ah ee degmooyinka wuxuu beddeli karaa xogta ku meel gaarka ah marka MoCIT soo diro  

Kuwan kama jiraan tijaabada hadda ilaa Wasaaraddu weydiisato.

## 7. Tijaabada la soo jeedinayo (2 daqiiqo)

**Ku bilow yar:** hal gobol — Maroodi Jeex.

1. Maamuluhu wuxuu eegaa dashboard, khariidad, warbixino, frequenci, iyo isticmaalayaal.
2. Hal kormeere ayaa tablet ku gudbiya kormeer dhab ah; maamulaha hawlgalka ama maamuluhu wuu oggolaadaa.
3. Socodsii hal codsi dadweyne: Madaxa Qaybta → isku-duwe → Agaasime → Agaasimaha Guud (ama isticmaal faylasha la beeray).
4. Hoggaanku wuxuu daabacaa sawir khariidad ah, PDF warbixin, iyo warqadda oggolaanshaha.
5. Ururi jawaab-celin 1–2 toddobaad, kadib ballaadhi gobol-gobol.

**Tallaabada xigta ee martigelinta:** ku rar server dawladeed (Apache/Nginx + PHP + MySQL). Faahfaahinta server-ka waa in MoCIT IT xaqiijiso.
